TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - Irrigation Subsidy Advisory

WHAT IS PMKSY
A national mission with the goal "Har Khet Ko Pani" (water to every farm), aimed at expanding assured irrigation, improving on-farm water-use efficiency, and increasing crop productivity per unit of water ("Per Drop More Crop"). It brings together large infrastructure projects (canals, dams) with individual farm-level micro-irrigation support.

KEY COMPONENT FOR INDIVIDUAL FARMERS: PER DROP MORE CROP (PDMC)
This is the part of the scheme most relevant to individual farmers - it provides subsidy support for installing micro-irrigation systems (drip and sprinkler) on their farms. Since 2022, PDMC operates as a distinct component focused specifically on farm-level water efficiency.

SUBSIDY AMOUNT
- Small and marginal farmers: 55% subsidy on micro-irrigation system cost
- Other farmers: 45% subsidy
- Some sources indicate subsidy can range up to 55-75% depending on category and state-specific top-up support - always confirm exact current rate with your state agriculture department, as states sometimes add their own additional subsidy on top of the central rate
- Cost is shared between Centre and State governments in 60:40 ratio for general states

BENEFITS OF DRIP/SPRINKLER IRRIGATION
- Saves 30-50% water compared to traditional flood irrigation
- Improves fertilizer efficiency (can be combined with fertigation - applying fertilizer through the irrigation system)
- Increases yield per unit of water used
- Reduces water-logging and associated disease risk compared to flood irrigation

HOW TO INSTALL
Farmers can install the micro-irrigation system themselves or through any approved micro-irrigation company/contractor empanelled under the scheme.

RECENT SCHEME UPDATES (as of 2026)
The scheme has been extended with a total outlay of Rs. 93,068.56 crore for the 2021-2026 period. The Union Budget 2026-27 allocated Rs. 6,587 crore specifically to fast-track micro-irrigation adoption among small and marginal farmers. A newer sub-component called M-CADWM (Modernization of Command Area Development and Water Management) is replacing older field channels with pressurized piped grids and micro-irrigation links in select project areas.

OTHER COMPONENTS OF PMKSY (broader, mostly infrastructure-level, less direct individual farmer action needed)
- Jal Sanchay (rainwater harvesting) and Jal Sinchan (precision water application) - the two broad operational streams
- Watershed Development Component - develops rainfed areas through watershed projects, soil conservation, and water harvesting, implemented by the Department of Land Resources
- Accelerated Irrigation Benefit Programme (AIBP) - large canal/dam infrastructure completion, not individual-farmer facing

HOW TO CHECK STATUS / APPLY
- Official portal: pmksy.gov.in
- Check application status using registration number or Aadhaar
- For help, call Kisan Call Centre toll-free: 1800-180-1551

IMPORTANT NOTE
All PMKSY services and subsidy applications are free through official channels. Never pay an unauthorized agent for application assistance. Only work with government-approved/empanelled micro-irrigation companies for installation to ensure your subsidy claim is valid.

SOURCE: Official PMKSY Portal (pmksy.gov.in), Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
