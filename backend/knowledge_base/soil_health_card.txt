TITLE: Soil Health Card Scheme - Farmer Advisory

WHAT IS SOIL HEALTH CARD (SHC)
A government scheme that provides farmers with free soil testing and a personalized report on their soil's nutrient status, along with recommendations on which fertilizers to use and in what quantity. Available across all 28 states and 8 union territories.

WHO CAN APPLY
- Small, marginal, and tenant farmers, or sharecroppers
- Any cultivable landholding, no minimum size required
- Documents needed: Aadhaar, land records (Khasra/Khatauni), PM-KISAN ID (optional)

BENEFITS
- Higher yields: 10-15% yield boost through optimized fertilizer use
- Cost savings: reduces fertilizer costs by 20-30% (roughly Rs. 2,000/hectare) by avoiding over-fertilization
- Guides crop selection based on soil condition (e.g., recommending pulses in low-nitrogen soil)
- No repayment needed - this is a free direct support service, not a loan

HOW TO APPLY
1. Visit official portal: soilhealth.dac.gov.in
2. Register using Aadhaar number and mobile number (OTP verification)
3. Fill in land details (Khasra/Khatauni) and bank details
4. Upload required documents
5. Soil sample is collected and tested; report delivered digitally or via CSC

CHECKING STATUS
- Check status via the official portal using registration number or Aadhaar
- Or call the Kisan Call Centre: 1800-180-1551

IMPORTANT
This is 100% free. No fees should be paid to any agent for registration or soil testing.

SOURCE: Official Soil Health Card Portal (soilhealth.dac.gov.in), Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
