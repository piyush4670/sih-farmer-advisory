TITLE: Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme - Farmer Advisory

WHAT IS KCC
A government-backed credit scheme launched in 1998 by the Government of India with RBI and NABARD, giving farmers flexible, revolving credit for agriculture and allied activities (dairy, fisheries, poultry, etc.) through a single-window facility, similar to a credit card - farmers borrow and repay as per their crop cycle.

INTEREST RATE
- Base interest rate: 7% per annum
- With government interest subvention (2%) plus Prompt Repayment Incentive (up to 3%), the effective rate can be as low as 4% per annum for short-term crop loans up to Rs. 3 lakh, IF repaid on time
- Important: repaying even one day late can result in losing the combined subvention benefit, pushing the rate back toward 7%

LOAN LIMIT
- Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 1.60 lakh without any security
- Loan limit is calculated based on land area, crop type, Scale of Finance (SOF) set by the district committee, and post-harvest expenses
- The credit limit ceiling has been raised up to Rs. 5 lakh under enhanced interest subvention (Union Budget 2025-26)

WHO CAN APPLY
- Owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers (even without land ownership, via self-declaration of cultivation)
- Landless farmers can apply through Joint Liability Groups
- PM-KISAN beneficiaries get priority for KCC issuance - banks are instructed to issue KCC to registered PM-KISAN beneficiaries who don't already have one

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Application form, 2 passport photos
- ID proof (Aadhaar/Driving License/Voter ID/Passport)
- Address proof
- Proof of landholding certified by revenue authorities
- Cropping pattern details (crops grown with acreage)
- Security documents (only required for loan limits above Rs. 1.60 lakh)

ADDITIONAL BENEFITS
- KCC holders get automatic accidental insurance coverage
- Compensation: Rs. 50,000 for accidental death/permanent disability, Rs. 25,000 for loss of one limb/eye (natural death and self-inflicted injury are excluded)
- If an accident occurs, the nominee/legal heir must inform the bank within 45 days
- Loanee farmers under KCC also get automatic PMFBY crop insurance coverage

BEST TIME TO APPLY
- Kharif (monsoon crop) window: April 1 to May 15
- Rabi (winter crop) window: September onwards
Applying within these windows ensures funds are ready before the season starts.

HOW TO APPLY
Visit any bank, cooperative bank, or Regional Rural Bank (RRB) with the required documents. Can also apply online through the bank's portal or Jan Samarth/Kisan Rin portals.

IMPORTANT WARNING
Misuse of KCC funds for non-agricultural purposes can lead to loan recall, interest charged at the full rate, and possible legal action.

SOURCE: RBI/NABARD guidelines, Government of India Kisan Credit Card Scheme documentation.
